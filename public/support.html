Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Everything works as a guest; your games stay on your device. An account is only needed to sync your handicap and join groups.

I forgot my password. Open Profile, tap “Forgot password?” and we’ll email you a reset link.

How do I delete my account? Open Profile → Delete account, enter your password and confirm. Your account and cloud data are deleted immediately.

Can I move my games to a new phone? Sign in and link yourself to your player; your synced games and handicap follow your account.
