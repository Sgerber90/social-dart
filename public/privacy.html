Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Social Dart (“the app”) is a darts scorer published by SJG Enterprises (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles on the web at social-dart.vercel.app and in the iOS app.

Playing as a guest

You can use the app without an account. Players, games, statistics and settings are stored only on your device (browser local storage). We do not receive them.

If you create an account (optional)

Accounts let you sync your handicap and games and join groups. When you create one we store:

  • Email address and password – handled by Google Firebase Authentication to sign you in. We never see your password.
  • Display name – shown on your public profile and to members of groups you join.
  • Gameplay data – games you choose to sync, your handicap, game and win counts (Google Cloud Firestore).
  • Groups – groups you create or join, your role and join requests.

Your display name, handicap, games played and wins form a public profile that other users can see. Synced game records are visible only to you.

We use this data only to provide the app’s features. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics.

Service providers

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), project hosted by Google Cloud. The website is hosted by Vercel. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your sign-in, public profile, synced games, group memberships and groups you own. Data on your device stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing info@sjgenterprises.co.za.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information (including under POPIA in South Africa and the GDPR where applicable) by contacting us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
