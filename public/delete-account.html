Delete your Social Dart account

Social Dart by SJG Enterprises · Last updated: 22 October 2026

Playing as a guest needs no account; guest data stays on your device and is removed when you clear app data or uninstall. If you created an account you can delete it and all associated data at any time.

Option 1 – delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open Social Dart (Android, iOS or social-dart.vercel.app) and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and scroll to Delete account.
  3. Enter your current password, type DELETE to confirm and tap Delete account.

Option 2 – ask us by email

Email info@sjgenterprises.co.za from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Social Dart account deletion”. We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address and password credential)
  • Your public profile (display name, handicap, games played and wins)
  • Synced games and statistics
  • Group memberships, join requests and groups you own

What is kept

Nothing is retained after deletion, except data already stored only on your own device (guest players and games), which you can remove by clearing app data or uninstalling. Encrypted backups maintained by our provider (Google Firebase) roll over within their standard retention period.
